Choose a sign-in method for the Viorant app you use:

| App | Sign-in methods |
| --- | --- |
| [Sentinel](https://sentinel.viorant.io/signin), your account website | Google, GitHub, or email and password |
| [`vio`, the CLI](/cli/sign-in) | Google, GitHub, or email and password |
| Viorant Hub, the desktop app | Google for now |

## Sign in to Sentinel

Open [Sentinel](https://sentinel.viorant.io/signin). Choose how to sign in:

- **Google or GitHub:** Select the matching button and sign in through your provider.
- **Email:** Enter your email and password. Complete the browser check. Click **Sign in**.

GitHub sign-in needs a verified email address on your GitHub account. If GitHub has
no verified email, verify an address in GitHub or choose another sign-in method.

### Create an account with email

Email account creation happens on Sentinel. The CLI signs in to an existing account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the account form">
    On [Sentinel's sign-in page](https://sentinel.viorant.io/signin), click **Create an account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Enter your email and a password with at least 12 characters.
    Complete the browser check. Click **Create account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Open the confirmation link in the same browser and profile where you created the account.
    The link confirms your email and returns you to Sentinel.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### If the confirmation email does not arrive

Look in your spam folder and make sure that you entered the correct address.
On the **Check your inbox** page, complete the browser check. Click **Resend email**.
After a resend, wait for the countdown before you request another email.

If you left that page, sign in with your email and password again.
If a confirmation link expires or was already used, sign in the same way.
An unconfirmed account returns to the confirmation page, where you can request another email.
If your email is already confirmed, you can continue without another confirmation.

### Reset your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request a reset link">
    On [Sentinel's sign-in page](https://sentinel.viorant.io/signin), click **Forgot password?**
    Enter your email. Complete the browser check. Click **Send reset link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new password">
    Open the email link in the same browser and profile where you requested it.
    Enter a new password with at least 12 characters. Click **Update password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in again">
    Use your email and new password on Sentinel or with `vio login --email`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Keep the browser window open until you finish. If an email link opens in another browser,
  copy the link into the browser that requested it. If that link fails, request a fresh link there.
  Keep confirmation and reset links private.
</Note>

The reset page shows the same response whether an account exists for that email or not.
If no email arrives, make sure that you entered the address for your Viorant account.
If a reset link expires or was already used, request a new link through **Forgot password?**

## Sign in to the Hub

The Hub uses **Google** for now. Sign-in happens in your browser, which returns you to the desktop app.

### Sign in with Google

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click “Sign in with Google”">
    On first launch, the Hub opens your default browser to Google's sign-in page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your account">
    Pick the Google account you want to use and approve access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to the Hub">
    The browser hands you back to the Hub automatically and you land in the app,
    signed in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your browser asks whether to open Viorant, allow it — that's the handoff step
  bringing you back into the Hub.
</Note>

### Multiple accounts on one Mac

Each signed-in user gets their own private workspace on the device — your agents,
prompts, and settings are isolated per account. Signing out keeps your data on disk;
signing back in restores it.
