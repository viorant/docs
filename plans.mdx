Your plan sets your credit allowance, managed model tiers, minimum schedule interval,
and deployed-agent limits. Prices below are in USD.

| Plan allowance | Free | Starter | Pro | Max |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $15/month | $50/month | $200/month |
| Credit allowance | 2,500 once | 10,000/month | 60,000/month | 350,000/month |
| Managed model tiers | Low, Medium | Low, Medium | Low, Medium, High | Low, Medium, High |
| Minimum schedule interval | 1 day | 1 hour | 5 minutes | 1 minute |
| Deployed agents on Viorant | 3 | 5 | 10 | 25 |
| Deployed agents on your infrastructure | — | — | 15 | 100 |

The dash means that deployment on your infrastructure is not included in that plan.
The two deployment limits are separate.

## Credits and model tiers

Free includes a one-time grant of 2,500 credits. Starter, Pro, and Max include a
monthly credit allowance. [One-time credit packs](/providers/credits#buying-more-credits)
are separate purchases and do not change your plan or its limits.

Low and Medium are available on every plan. High is available on Pro and Max.
The CLI calls Medium `mid`. See [managed model tiers](/providers/credits#tiers).

## Schedules

The minimum interval applies to scheduled runs and is enforced by Viorant.
A schedule that is too frequent for your plan is refused. If a plan change makes
an existing schedule too frequent, Viorant pauses it.

Max allows schedules every minute. The CLI in `vio` 1.0.7 accepts minute intervals
from `5m` to `59m`, so that version cannot set a one-minute schedule.
See [CLI schedule options](/cli/schedule#set-a-schedule).

## View or change your plan

Your current plan is shown in [Sentinel](https://sentinel.viorant.io/plans).
[`vio upgrade`](/cli/sign-in#your-plan) opens that page from the terminal.

<Note>
  `vio` 1.0.7 can display Max as Pro for compatibility. Your Max allowances still
  apply. Check Sentinel for your current plan.
</Note>
