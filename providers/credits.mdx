You don't need an API key to start using Viorant. Every signed-in account gets a
**credit wallet** and access to the **Viorant managed provider**, so you can write a
prompt, run an agent, and see real output on your first session.

<Note>
  Credits are the zero-setup path. [Bringing your own key](/providers/byok) is still
  fully supported and has no credit cost — use it when you want your own account,
  your own rate limits, or a model Viorant doesn't manage.
</Note>

## Your wallet

New accounts are granted **2,500 credits**, once. There's no card, no subscription,
and no monthly reset.

The wallet is a single balance. Everything you do that calls a managed model draws
from the same pool — there's no separate allowance per feature.

from your machine straight to the vendor and never touches Viorant's metering.

## Tiers

The Viorant provider offers two tiers. They differ in capability and in what they
cost you per call:

| Tier | Use it for | Relative cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Medium** | Everyday work — the default, and the right starting point | Moderate |
| **Low** | Fast, cheap iteration and simple prompts | Lowest |

Pick a tier wherever you choose a Viorant model — when you add one to a Space's model
pool, in the prompt builder when you generate, or in the
[Assistant](/concepts/assistant). A single prompt generation typically costs **a credit
or two on Low** and **under ten on Medium**, so the starting grant covers a lot of
ordinary iteration.

<Tip>
  Cost scales with how much text goes in and comes out, not just the tier. A long
  document in the context window costs more than a one-line question at the same
  tier.
</Tip>

## Watching your balance

Your balance is visible in the Hub next to your profile, and the prompt builder shows
what a generation will draw before you run it.

For the detail, open the profile menu and choose **Usage**. You'll get your current
balance plus a day-by-day history — each date shows the balance you ended that day on,
and expands to the individual entries behind it, so you can see exactly what a
particular session cost.

## Buying more credits

You can top up at any time at [sentinel.viorant.io](https://sentinel.viorant.io) — sign in
with the same account you use in the Hub, open **Billing**, and pick a pack. The Hub's
credit surfaces link straight there.

| Pack | Price | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $10 | 10,000 |
| Builder | $50 | 52,500 |
| Scale | $100 | 110,000 |
| Agent Fleet | $500 | 575,000 |

**Purchased credits never expire**, and they add to whatever you already have rather than
replacing it. There's no subscription and no monthly reset.

<Note>
  The $10 pack is exactly 10,000 credits — the published rate, with no bonus. The larger
  packs include progressively more credits per dollar, which is why their totals aren't
  round multiples.
</Note>

Past purchases are listed under Billing, alongside your day-by-day usage.

## When you run out

At zero, calls to the Viorant provider stop rather than silently failing or billing
you. The Hub tells you the balance is exhausted, and you can either
[top up](https://sentinel.viorant.io) or [add your own key](/providers/byok).

Adding a key restores full functionality immediately — your own models have no credit
cost and no cap beyond whatever your provider account allows.

## Where your data goes

Managed-model calls are **metered server-side**, which means the request passes through
Viorant's control plane to reach the model. That's what makes zero-key usage possible.

If you would rather no prompt content passed through Viorant at all,
[use your own key](/providers/byok) — that path calls the provider directly from your
machine, and Viorant is not in the request path.
