Every account is on a **plan**. Your plan sets how many [credits](/providers/credits)
you're granted, which model tiers you can use, and how many agents you can have
deployed at once.

## Compare plans

| | **Free** | **Starter** | **Pro** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Price | $0 | $15 / month | $50 / month |
| Credits | 2,500, once | 10,000 every month | 60,000 every month |
| Model tiers | Low, Medium | Low, Medium | Low, Medium, **High** |
| Deployed agents | 3 | 5 | 10 |

New accounts start on **Free**. There's no card required and nothing to cancel.

[Top-up packs](/providers/credits#buying-more-credits) work on every plan, including
Free. Purchased credits add to your balance and never expire.

## Subscribe or upgrade

Plans are managed in Sentinel, Viorant's web dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plans page">
    Go to [sentinel.viorant.io/plans](https://sentinel.viorant.io/plans) and sign in with
    the same account you use in the Hub.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Pick **Starter** or **Pro**. You pay on a secure Stripe checkout page — Viorant never
    sees your card details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the grant">
    Your plan's credits are added once the payment clears. Until then the plans page
    shows the subscription as pending rather than guessing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change or cancel

Once you're subscribed, choose **Manage subscription** on the
[plans page](https://sentinel.viorant.io/plans). It opens the Stripe customer portal,
where you change plan, update your card, see invoices, or cancel.

- **Upgrades take effect immediately.** You're invoiced for the change straight away,
  and your balance gets the difference between the two plans' monthly credits.
- **Downgrades and cancellations take effect at the end of the current period.** You
  keep your current plan until then. Cancelling returns you to Free.
- If a payment fails, you keep your plan's features while it's retried, but no new
  monthly credits are granted until an invoice is paid.

If you have more agents deployed than a lower plan allows, the ones already running keep
running — you just can't deploy another until you're under the limit.

## Where you see your plan

**In the Hub:**

- **Profile** shows your plan and its credit allowance.
- **Usage** shows your plan, its allowance, and when it renews.
- **Deployments** shows how many agents you have deployed against your plan's limit —
  for example *2 of 3 · Free*.

**In Sentinel**, [the plans page](https://sentinel.viorant.io/plans) shows your current
plan and when it renews or ends.

Requests for a model tier your plan doesn't include are refused. Most tier pickers in
the Hub show such a tier as locked, with an **Upgrade** link.
